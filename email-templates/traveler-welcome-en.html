🌎 HOW DOES IT WORK?

  1. Choose your destination. Search for personal tour guides by country or city.
  2. Find your ideal guide. Compare languages, experience, availability, profiles, and rates.
  3. Book and pay securely. Select the service that best suits your plans and pay within I GUIDE U.
  4. Plan with your guide. Once payment is confirmed, a private chat opens within the app so you can arrange your meeting time, discuss possible itineraries, and tailor the experience to your preferences.
  5. Enjoy your experience. Your guide will accompany you during your booked service so you can discover your destination in a more personal way.

💳 WHAT DOES YOUR BOOKING INCLUDE?

The amount paid covers your personal tour guide’s services for the booked hours, whether hourly, an 8-hour day, or 24-hour accompaniment.

Meals, drinks, admission tickets, transportation, travel fares, accommodation, and other additional expenses — for both you and your guide — are not included, unless expressly stated otherwise.

I GUIDE U does not manage or guarantee payment of additional expenses outside the booking made through the platform. The service start time must be agreed with your guide and recorded in the private I GUIDE U chat.
