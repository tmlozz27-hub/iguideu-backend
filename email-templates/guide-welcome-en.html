🌎 HOW DOES I GUIDE U WORK?

I GUIDE U promotes the use of personal tour guides worldwide, helping more travelers enjoy personalized experiences during their trips.

Our platform gives travelers the freedom to choose the personal tour guide they wish to hire, based on their interests, language, experience, availability, and rates.

You offer your knowledge, experience, and services. Travelers choose you.

I GUIDE U connects travelers with independent guides and facilitates bookings, payments, and communication through the platform. The personal tour guide service is provided by the hired guide, not directly by I GUIDE U.

1. THE TRAVELER CHOOSES THE DATE AND DURATION

The traveler selects the booking date and the number of hours they wish to book.

The starting time is agreed upon later between the traveler and guide through I GUIDE U’s private chat, once the booking is confirmed and payment has been made.

Both parties must record the agreed starting time in the chat to avoid misunderstandings.

2. YOU CHOOSE HOW TO OFFER YOUR SERVICES

You can offer your services according to your availability:

Hourly: shorter tours and experiences. 8-hour day: accompanying the traveler for a full day. 24-hour service: accompanying the traveler for an entire day, when you are available to provide this service.

You set your rates and availability in your profile.

3. RATES BASED ON THE TRAVELER’S AGE

Guides must follow the age-based pricing conditions and differences shown on the I GUIDE U booking page.

Before accepting a booking, review the applicable conditions and details of the service booked.

4. ADDITIONAL EXPENSES ARE NOT INCLUDED

The amount paid by the traveler covers the booked hours of personal tour guide service.

Meals, drinks, admission tickets, transportation, fares, accommodation, and other additional expenses — for both the traveler and the guide — are not included, unless expressly stated otherwise.

This also applies to 8-hour days and 24-hour accompaniment services.

I GUIDE U does not manage or guarantee payment of additional expenses outside the booking made through the platform.

5. BOOKING CONFIRMATION

When you receive a booking request, review the date, duration, and service conditions before confirming it.

Keep your availability up to date to avoid bookings you cannot fulfill.

Cancellations and refunds are subject to I GUIDE U’s current terms and conditions.

6. COMMUNICATION WITH THE TRAVELER

Once payment is confirmed, use I GUIDE U’s private chat to coordinate the meeting, agree on the starting time, plan the itinerary, and record important arrangements related to the booked service.

Bookings and payments for the guide service must be made through I GUIDE U to preserve transaction records and the protections provided by the platform.

💳 7. MEMBERSHIP, COMMISSIONS, AND GUIDE PAYMENTS

I GUIDE U has two types of charges:

1. MONTHLY MEMBERSHIP

Guides have a monthly membership fee that varies according to the country where they offer their services.

Your membership will be 100% WAIVED until you complete your first service and for the following 3 months!

You will not have to pay the membership fee during this period.

Once the waived period ends, the monthly fee for your country will apply, according to the current terms.

2. BOOKING COMMISSION

For every service booked and paid for through I GUIDE U:

90% goes to the personal tour guide.

10% goes to I GUIDE U as a transaction commission.

The commission is calculated on the amount paid for the booked service hours.

3. WHEN WILL YOU RECEIVE YOUR PAYMENT?

The guide payment becomes eligible for processing 48 hours after the service ends, subject to the applicable checks.

Taxes, withholding, bank charges, and settlement times imposed by banks or financial intermediaries depend on the relevant institutions and regulations and are not part of I GUIDE U’s commission.

Each guide is responsible for complying with their applicable tax obligations.

8. BANK DETAILS TO RECEIVE YOUR PAYMENTS

To receive payments, you must provide your bank details through the secure process made available by I GUIDE U.

Register my bank details

Sign in with your I GUIDE U account. Never send bank details by email.

Make sure your bank details are accurate and up to date to avoid transfer issues.

Never share passwords or bank login credentials through the chat.

9. YOUR PROFESSIONAL COMMITMENT

As a personal tour guide, it is important that you:

Keep information about your experience, languages, and knowledge accurate and up to date. Honor the agreed service conditions, duration, and times. Comply with local regulations applicable to your work. Provide professional, responsible service tailored to each traveler’s preferences.
